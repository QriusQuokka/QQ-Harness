# Product Sense

> Product principles and judgment calls that shape what we build and how we prioritize.
> **Frame only.** Onboard the agent to product norms the way you would a new teammate.

## Principles (template)
- Who is the user, and what is the core job-to-be-done?
- What are we explicitly NOT building?
- How do we trade off speed vs. polish for this product?

## Acceptance-criteria philosophy
User feedback is converted into **acceptance criteria** (testable success conditions), which
flow into `product-specs/` and become the deterministic checks the verification stage binds
to. A feature isn't "done" because it looks right — it's done when its acceptance criteria
are verified.
